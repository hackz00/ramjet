# Notice and attribution

Ramjet began as a fork of [Scramjet](https://github.com/MercuryWorkshop/scramjet), by Mercury Workshop and its contributors, at commit b14b709a (version 2.0.67-alpha.2).

Scramjet supplied the interception and rewriting architecture, Rust JavaScript rewriter, browser runtime and much of the regression suite. Ramjet adds streaming HTML rewriting, persistent output caching, an assisted transport, startup changes and benchmark tooling. Measurements are recorded in [docs/perf/RESULTS.md](docs/perf/RESULTS.md).

Third-party dependencies, including Mercury Workshop's Wisp and browser transports, retain their own names and licenses. Upstream copyright and license notices remain with their code.
